Villager

Raise a settlement from timber and earth. Gather wood, food, gold and stone. Train soldiers. Advance an age. Outlast the red, yellow and green hosts — and raid the pale villages for tribute.

  • Left click selects a unit or building. Drag to box-select.
  • Right click empty ground to move, a resource to gather, an enemy to attack.
  • Houses raise population. A mill and farms keep food coming after the berries are gone.
  • Advance to the Iron Age for better farms, archers, and stronger towers.
