MAYHEM CLEANER

JANITORIAL DIVISION · SHIFT 07

Somebody made a mess. You have a mop, a pair of hands, a water bucket, a bin dispenser, and an incinerator. Do not clock out until the room is clean.

  • Click — capture mouse / look around
  • WASD — walk
  • 1 — equip mop   2 — equip hands
  • Left click — mop, pick up, rinse, place, or dump
  • Mop holds 5 wipes. A dirty mop smears. Rinse it in the blue bucket.
  • Hands can carry the bucket, trash, or a bin. Switching to the mop sets it down.
  • Click the gray dispenser to pop out a bin. Fill it, then empty it at the incinerator.
  • Walk through blood and your shoes track prints. Mop those too.
  • The table radio in the far corner plays a looping station. Click to toggle.
  • Esc — release mouse
